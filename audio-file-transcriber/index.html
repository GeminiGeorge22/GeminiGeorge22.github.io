Audio to Text Transcriber: Whisper Speech to Text

Give it links to audio or video files. It returns the transcript, timed segments, and SRT and VTT subtitles. OpenAI's open Whisper model runs inside the Actor, so your audio is not sent to an outside speech API.

Open Audio to Text Transcriber on Apify →

The one question it answers

What are the words in these audio or video files, with timestamps and ready-made SRT or VTT subtitles?

Who it is for

Podcasters, researchers and journalists who need audio to text for recordings, interviews and meetings; AI agents and workflows that need the words in an mp3 or wav link; anyone turning video files into subtitles.

This Actor transcribes files you link to. It does not download Instagram, TikTok or YouTube posts.

Input

FieldWhat to putDefault
audioUrlsDirect links to audio or video files (mp3, wav, m4a, ogg, flac, webm, mp4 and other formats ffmpeg reads). Up to 300 MB per file.Store example: the public JFK wav sample
modelWhisper tiny, base, or small. The price per minute is the same for every model.base
languageTwo-letter code (en, fr, es, de …), or leave empty to detect itdetect automatically
translateToEnglishOutput an English translation instead of the original-language transcriptfalse
skipSilenceSkip long silent stretches before transcribingtrue

Output

One dataset row per file, with the full text, timed segments, srt and vtt subtitles, detected language, and duration. Whisper runs inside the Actor — audio is not sent to an outside speech API. Pay per event on your own Apify account (free tier available). Current rates are on the Store page. No speaker labels (diarization).

Try it in 1 minute

  1. Open the Store page and run the example input (the short public JFK wav, Whisper Base).
  2. Or pin it in an MCP client: mcp.apify.com?tools=publicrecords/audio-file-transcriber and sign in with your Apify account.
  3. Or import the n8n workflow or the Make HTTP recipe. Set your own Apify token; do not commit it.
