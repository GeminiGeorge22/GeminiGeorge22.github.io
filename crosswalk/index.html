Company Socials (Crosswalk)

Crosswalk resolves a company's LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube from its website or name. Links that pass a platform check carry confidence and evidence; Instagram and Facebook come from what the company lists on its own site. Organizations and places only — never people, never guesses.

What Crosswalk is for

SDR and RevOps teams use it to attach verified company socials to lead lists. Analysts use it to map a brand's public presence. Agents call it once per company to get a stable canonicalId before other tools run.

Give a domain, a profile URL, a ticker, or a company name. Enrich another Actor's dataset (Google Maps, jobs, company scrapers) so every row comes back with a Crosswalk block. Cache hits for well-known companies are free.

What you can rely on

  • A link is verified only when that record's own platform check passed (profile points back at the company domain), with evidence on the row.
  • Instagram and Facebook links are the ones the company lists on its own website; we don't confirm them with the platforms on the default path.
  • Organizations and places only — person profiles are refused.
  • If the name is ambiguous, you get candidates instead of a silent pick.
  • Stable canonicalId; rebrand and handle-change events when the graph notices them.

Frequently asked

How do I find a company's LinkedIn page from its website?
Give Crosswalk the domain. It reads the profiles the company declares on its site and in Wikidata, then checks that the LinkedIn page points back at the domain.
Does it return people?
No. Person profiles and personal websites are refused. Organizations and places only.
What does it cost?
Pay per resolved organization; cache hits are free and the graph is pre-seeded with well-known companies.
