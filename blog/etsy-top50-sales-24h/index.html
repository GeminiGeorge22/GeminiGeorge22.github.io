How the ranking works

  • delta_last — sales since the previous read (the last-interval / ~24h sales delta).
  • sales_per_day — that change divided by read_interval_days (measured, not modelled).
  • Eligible shops need history_days ≥ 2 so those deltas exist. Vintage/reset rows that suppress deltas are excluded.
  • Sort by delta_last descending (tie-break sales_per_day, then sales_count), cap at 50. No padding.

A breakout column appears in the CSV only after the panel has history_days_max ≥ 7. This edition’s max is 3, so breakout columns are omitted.
