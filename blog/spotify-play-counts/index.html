How to scrape Spotify play counts and monthly listeners

A short path for music marketers who need play counts and monthly listeners from a public Spotify link. Author: Public Records (I maintain the Actor).

What you get

The Spotify Play Count Scraper takes a track, album, playlist or artist URL and returns every track's play count, plus an artist's monthly listeners and followers. Optional streams per day. No Spotify login. Pay per track.

1. Run the Store example

On the Store page, keep the example album URL (or paste your own public link), set maxTracksPerTarget if you want a cap, and start a run on your Apify account. The free tier is enough to try it.

2. Or pin it in MCP

Paste https://mcp.apify.com?tools=publicrecords/spotify-play-count-scraper into Claude, Cursor, or another MCP client and sign in with your own Apify account.

3. Or schedule it in n8n or Make

Import templates/n8n/spotify-play-count-scraper.json and add a Header Auth credential Authorization: Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN>. The Make HTTP recipe is in templates/make.

POST https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/publicrecords~spotify-play-count-scraper/run-sync-get-dataset-items
Authorization: Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN>
{"urls":["https://open.spotify.com/album/4aawyAB9vmqN3uQ7FjRGTy"],"maxTracksPerTarget":50}
