Scrape Etsy search results into listing rows (your proxy)

2026-10-02

Disclosure: I publish and maintain the Actor this tutorial uses (publicrecords/etsy-search-scraper on Apify).

You have a search query — a niche keyword, a product type, a competitor phrase — and you need structured listing rows (title, price, shop, badges), not a screenshot of page one. Opening tabs by hand does not leave a dataset you can filter, score, or join to a shop tracker.

The method

  1. Hit Etsy search with a real residential proxy. Search HTML is challenged often; datacenter IPs commonly fail. Use Apify RESIDENTIAL (or your own residential proxy). When every page is challenged, the Actor exits blocked_on_your_proxy and charges no listing rows.
  2. Parse listing cards into one row per listing. Fields include listing_id, url, title, price, currency, shop_name / shop_url, rating / review counts, and badges (bestseller, popular_now, free_shipping, etc.), plus page, position, and query.
  3. Paginate under a hard cap. Set maxPages (and optionally maxItems) so a warm proxy hang cannot run forever. Incomplete cards (missing price/title/shop) are pushed free and never billed.
  4. Bill only delivered complete rows. PPE is actor-start once + listing-row per complete listing. Blocked pages charge nothing.
  5. Join to shop velocity when you care about the seller, not only the card. Listing rows give you the shop name; the companion Actor publicrecords/etsy-shop-velocity turns shop names into sales counters and rates (no proxy on that path).

Doing it for real (measured run)

Honesty on proxies (measured, not marketing)

The first attempt with the same input (9RfVZjBbBvCvbs8JX) FAILED after ~620 s with:

blocked_on_your_proxy: Etsy challenged every page on the configured proxy. Use Apify RESIDENTIAL (default) or a residential proxy of your own. No listing rows were charged.

Retry on RESIDENTIAL US (pRpdAMmfeMHdmuRXA) PASSED in ~53 s with 36 complete rows. That is the expected failure mode when the proxy is challenged: you pay the actor-start (if the run started under PPE) and zero listing-row charges for blocked pages. Prefer RESIDENTIAL; expect occasional warm hangs and retry.

Cost math you can recompute

  • 1 complete listing row: $0.005 + $0.006 = $0.011
  • 36 rows (this tutorial): $0.005 + 36×$0.006 = $0.221
  • 100 complete rows: $0.005 + 100×$0.006 = $0.605
