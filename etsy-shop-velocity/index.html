Etsy Shop Sales Tracker & Scraper

Track any Etsy shop's sales day by day with measured counters, 7- and 28-day changes, modelled rates and breakout flags. One row per shop, pay per shop. No proxy required for the hosted panel path.

Open Etsy Shop Sales Tracker on Apify →

The one question it answers

How fast is this Etsy shop selling — and did it just break out against its own recent history?

Input

FieldWhat to putDefault
shopsEtsy shop names (the part after etsy.com/shop/)optional
keywordsWords matched against shop title / headlineoptional
categoryPanel category (e.g. jewelry, home-decor)all
minSalesOnly shops at or above this sales counter0
breakoutOnlyReturn only breakout-flagged shopsfalse
maxShopsUpper bound on rows returned (and charged)200

Output

One dataset item per shop:

shop shop_url as_of sales_count sales_precision delta_last sales_per_day delta_7d delta_28d units_day breakout reviews_count rating admirers listings_active history_days snapshot_date snapshot_stale

Velocity and breakout fields stay null until enough history exists (breakouts need at least 7 days). A counter that could not be read is null, never 0.

How it works

  • Serves rows from a hosted panel of public Etsy shop counters — no proxy configuration required for this path.
  • Named shops not yet in the panel are added on the next capture.
  • Runs are pay-per-event: actor-start once plus shop-row per shop returned.
