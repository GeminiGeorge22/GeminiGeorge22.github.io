Email Verifier: Bulk Email Validation, MX & Disposable Check

Paste a list of email addresses and get a verdict for each one, without sending any mail. It checks the domain and its MX records, not the mailbox. This is not a mailbox verifier and it does not do an SMTP check.

Open Email Verifier on Apify →

The one question it answers

For each address: is the syntax valid, does the domain exist, does it have MX records, and is it a disposable domain, a role account, or a typo — without sending mail or checking the mailbox?

Who it is for

Anyone cleaning a list before a send, checking sign-up forms, or scoring leads, and AI agents that need a quick, cheap email check. It is not a mailbox-level or SMTP verifier.

What it checks — and what it does not

For every address the Actor checks the syntax, whether the domain exists, its MX records (or A-record fallback), SPF and DMARC, and whether it is a disposable domain, a role account (info@, support@) or a free webmail provider. It also suggests a fix for common domain typos such as gmial.com.

It does not check the mailbox and it does not send mail. There is no SMTP probe. A made-up name at a real domain can still get the verdict deliverable_domain, which only means the domain and its MX records can receive mail and nothing about the address looks risky.

Output

One row per address, with verdict (deliverable_domain, risky, or undeliverable), plain-language reasons, MX records, and flags for disposable, role account and free provider. Pay per event on your own Apify account (free tier available). Current rates are on the Store page.

The disposable list is the open disposable-email-domains blocklist (CC0), bundled with each build. This tool is not affiliated with Stripe, Apify, Mailinator or Google; those names appear only in the Store example or as public MX hosts.
