Grants.gov Finder

Grants.gov Finder searches open US federal grant opportunities on Grants.gov via the official search2 API and returns flat FOA rows — opportunity IDs, status, CFDA/ALN codes — with keyword, agency, posted-within, and date filters.

Open Grants.gov Opportunities on Apify →

Who uses it

Grant writers and consultants shortlist FOAs. University research offices watch agency slices. Nonprofits monitor posted-within windows without hand-copying the Grants.gov UI.

For fused SAM contracts + Grants with deltas in one schema, see the Federal Opportunity Feed hub. This Actor stays as a plain Grants.gov pull.

What you can rely on

  • Official Grants.gov search2 API.
  • Flat deduplicated FOA rows with opportunity identifiers, status, and CFDA/ALN codes.
  • Keyword, agency, posted-within, and date filters.
  • Optional synopsis and funding-instrument hydration.

Uses the Grants.gov API but is not endorsed or certified by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.
