The one question it answers

How many times has this track been played, and what are this artist's monthly listeners and followers?

Who it is for

Music marketers, managers, and analysts who need play counts and monthly listeners without a Spotify login.

Output

Every track's play count, plus an artist's monthly listeners and followers. Optional streams per day. No login. Pay per track. You run it on your own Apify account (free tier available).

Try it in 1 minute

  1. Open the Store page and paste a track, album, playlist or artist URL (the example uses a public album).
  2. Or pin it in an MCP client: mcp.apify.com?tools=publicrecords/spotify-play-count-scraper and sign in with your Apify account.
  3. Or import the n8n workflow or the Make HTTP recipe. Set your own Apify token; do not commit it.
